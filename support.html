Frequently asked questions

How do I import from Google Classroom?

On a computer, open Google Calendar, find your Classroom calendar, and copy its calendar link (the iCalendar / ".ics" address). In StudyTrack, open Settings (the gear on the Dashboard) → Google Classroom Sync, paste the link, and tap Sync Assignments. After that, the app re-syncs on its own.

Why don't I see reminders?

Make sure notifications are allowed for StudyTrack in iOS Settings, and that "Assignment reminders" is turned on in the app's Settings.

Does my data sync between devices?

Yes — if you're signed into iCloud, your assignments sync privately across your own devices. Your data is never shared with anyone else.

How do I delete my account or data?

Open Settings in the app and choose "Delete Account" to remove your account. You can delete individual assignments any time, or delete the app to remove all local data.
