StudyTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

StudyTrack is a homework and assignment tracker. In short: your data stays on your device and in your own private iCloud account. We do not collect it, see it, or share it with anyone.

Information the app stores

StudyTrack stores the following on your device (and, if you enable iCloud, in your own private iCloud account so it syncs across your devices):

  • Assignments you add or import — titles, classes/subjects, due dates, categories, priorities, and notes.
  • Grades and study information you choose to enter.
  • Your app settings and preferences.

This information is stored locally using Apple's SwiftData and, when available, Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). It is tied to your Apple ID and is not accessible to the developer.

Sign in with Apple

StudyTrack uses Sign in with Apple for its optional account. We receive only a private identifier (and your name, if you choose to share it) to personalize the app. We do not receive your Apple password, and there is no separate account server. You can remove this at any time with "Delete Account" in Settings.

Google Classroom calendar

If you choose to sync with Google Classroom, you paste a calendar link (an iCalendar ".ics" URL) into the app. The app downloads that calendar directly from Google to show your assignments and due dates. The link is stored on your device only. StudyTrack does not send this link, or the assignments it contains, to the developer or any third party.

Reminders and notifications

With your permission, StudyTrack can send you local notifications about upcoming assignments and create reminders in Apple's Reminders app. These stay on your device and within Apple's services. You can turn them off any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not operate any server that receives your data.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

Data deletion

You can delete any assignment or grade at any time in the app, remove your account with "Delete Account" in Settings, or delete the app to remove its local data. iCloud data is managed through your Apple ID settings.

Children's privacy

StudyTrack is intended for students. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, since it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
